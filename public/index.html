UNDER THE HOOD

Signals, not guesses.

01

Look every five minutes

We fetch each eligible public page and inspect its first-party JavaScript URLs, content hashes, and exposed build ID. Checks are spread out, with backoff for failures.

02

Confirm the change

The first successful check establishes a baseline. A different fingerprint must persist on a follow-up check before we record it. Previously seen fingerprints and rollbacks don't add to the count.

03

Keep the limits visible

These are observed frontend releases, not commits, backend deployments, or employee working hours. A/B tests and regional rollouts can still introduce noise. Multiple releases between checks can be missed.

04

Compare what we actually saw

Rankings use 7- or 30-day windows. New companies have shorter observation histories. Every profile shows its baseline date and successful checks. Weekend and hourly aggregates use UTC.

YC directory data comes from the unofficial yc-oss API. Active and public companies with usable URLs are eligible. Marketing websites may release independently of the product. We inspect script URLs and up to eight stable-name scripts per page; hashed script URLs are tracked without repeatedly downloading their contents. Lazy-loaded routes and third-party CDN assets can be missed.
